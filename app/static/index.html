Regulatory Standards & Chemical Safety Audit Protocols

Codified rules enforced automatically by the Gemini AI verification bridge to eliminate hallucinations and hazardous chemical poisoning.

❌ Strictly Prohibited / Banned Agrochemicals

  • Endosulfan: Banned persistent organic pollutant; causes severe neurotoxicity.
  • Monocrotophos: WHO Class Ib acute organophosphate toxin; banned on field crops.
  • Paraquat: Non-selective high-hazard herbicide with irreversible pulmonary toxicity.
  • Chlorpyrifos: Severely restricted neurotoxin; hazard to children and pollinators.

🌿 Approved Organic & Biological First-Line Controls

  • Neem Seed Kernel Extract (NSKE 5%): Safe bio-antifeedant & fungal spore suppressant.
  • Trichoderma viride (5g/L): Natural antagonistic bio-fungicide suppressing rust & root rot.
  • Pseudomonas fluorescens: Siderophore-producing bio-agent suppressing rice blast & blight.
  • Bordeaux Mixture (1%): Traditional verified copper sulfate & lime preventative.
