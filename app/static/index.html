Regulatory Standards & Chemical Safety Audit Protocols

Understanding Agricultural Safety Standards: Toxic, unverified chemicals poison drinking water, degrade soil fertility, kill honeybees, and cause severe farmer illnesses. Our AI enforces strict 'Organic-First' rules, rejecting dangerous poisons while recommending verified biological treatments.

Regulatory Standards & Chemical Safety Audit Protocols

❌ Strictly Prohibited / Banned Agrochemicals (Never Use These)

  • Endosulfan: Banned persistent neurotoxin; causes irreversible reproductive and central nervous system damage.
  • Monocrotophos: WHO Class Ib acute organophosphate toxin; strictly banned on field crops due to fatal accidental ingestion risks.
  • Paraquat: High-hazard non-selective herbicide with no known antidote; causes severe lung fibrosis.
  • Chlorpyrifos: Banned in modern agriculture; severely harms children, farm workers, pollinators, and beneficial earthworms.

🌿 Approved Organic & Biological First-Line Controls (Recommended)

  • Neem Seed Kernel Extract (NSKE 5%): Proven botanical bio-antifeedant and spore suppressant safe for mammals and pollinators.
  • Trichoderma viride (5g/L): Natural antagonistic bio-fungicide that outcompetes pathogenic root rot and rust fungi.
  • Pseudomonas fluorescens: Siderophore-producing bio-agent suppressing rice blast, bacterial leaf blight, and soil-borne wilt.
  • Bordeaux Mixture (1%): Traditional verified copper sulfate & lime preventative barrier safe for foliage when properly diluted.
